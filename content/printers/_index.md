Choose a brand below. Each brand has a permanent home for **all** of its setup and configuration guides, not just scanning. You do **not** need PrintMaster to use this knowledge base.

## Need scan-to-folder now?

Start with [Scanning & SMB](/scanning/): prepare the receiving PC, configure the copier, and verify delivery. The [brand-neutral copier checklist](/scanning/copier-setup/) is available now. Brand-specific pages distinguish **Documentation planned** from inspected walkthroughs marked **Delivery test pending**; check each page's model and verification scope.

## What belongs in a brand guide?

- Exact model or family, firmware, prerequisites, and required administrator access.
- A verified sequence with annotated, redacted screenshots.
- A test that proves the intended result, plus troubleshooting and rollback notes.
- Links back to shared concepts rather than repeated Windows, SMB, or mail-server instructions.

The same brand can have several unrelated interfaces. A future guide for one family will not imply support for all models.

Developer OID mappings and configuration investigations remain under [vendor research](/development/vendor/); they are not end-user setup procedures.
