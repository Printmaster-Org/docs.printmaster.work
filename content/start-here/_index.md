New to PrintMaster? Start with an **Agent** for one location, or a **Server plus Agents** for a fleet across multiple sites. The Agent discovers and monitors printers; the Server brings sites together.

1. **Choose and install** the components you need.
2. **Complete your first setup** and discover a small, authorized printer range.
3. Continue with [day-to-day fleet management](/guides/) or [production deployment and administration](/deployment/).

Only trying to get a copier scanning? You do **not** need PrintMaster: go directly to [Scanning & SMB](/scanning/) or [find your printer brand](/printers/).
